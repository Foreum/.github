# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Foreum aims to be a welcoming, respectful space for everyone who participates in the project, regardless of experience level, background, identity, or viewpoint.

We are committed to making participation in the project a harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Expected behaviour

Examples of behaviour that helps create a positive environment include:

- Being respectful and considerate.
- Giving constructive feedback.
- Critiquing ideas rather than attacking people.
- Assuming good intent while remaining open to correction.
- Respecting different perspectives and levels of experience.
- Keeping project discussions focused and professional.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, intimidation, doxxing, and publishing private information without permission.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies to project spaces, including issues, pull requests, discussions, and other spaces where someone is representing Foreum or its community.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, contact a project maintainer privately through GitHub. Do not use public issues to report sensitive incidents.

Reports will be reviewed as fairly and promptly as the project's capacity allows. The maintainer will respect the privacy of the reporter to the extent possible.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove or edit inappropriate content, close discussions, or restrict participation when necessary. Decisions will take into account the nature and severity of the behaviour.

## Attribution

This policy is inspired by the Contributor Covenant community code-of-conduct model. The Foreum-specific text above describes the project's expectations and reporting process.
